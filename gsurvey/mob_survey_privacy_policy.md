# Privacy Policy — AcreTrack: GPS Land Measure

Effective date: 2026-11-02
Developer contact:  rami.palamakula+play@gmail.com

AcreTrack ("the App") measures land distance, area, perimeter and circular area using GPS and maps.

## Data we collect
The developer does **not** operate any server and does **not** operate servers or sell your personal data. Third-party ad providers may collect data as described under Advertising.

## Data processed on your device
- **Precise/approximate location** (GPS): used to center the map, and for the "Walk & Add Points" feature. Used only while the App is open (no background location). It is not sent to the developer.
- **Saved surveys** (points, measurements, address text, snapshot image): stored locally in an on-device SQLite database. You can delete them anytime in the App; uninstalling removes them.
- **Exports** (PNG/PDF): created in temporary storage and shared only when you choose via the system share sheet.

## Third-party services
Data is sent to these services only as needed to provide features:
- **Google Maps SDK / Google Geocoding & Places-style lookups** — map tiles and coordinates are sent to Google. See https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Platform geocoding (Android Geocoder / Apple CLLocation)** — converts coordinates to an address and addresses to coordinates.
- **Share targets** (WhatsApp, etc.) — receive only what you choose to share (image, PDF, or a link containing plot coordinates).

## Shared links
"Share" creates a link containing the plot's coordinates. Anyone with the link can view that location. Share only with people you trust.

## Advertising
The App may display ads served by third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob). These providers may use the device advertising ID and collect usage/device data to show and measure ads, subject to their policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

## Children
The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Security
Data stays on your device. No account or login is required.

## Your choices
Revoke location permission in system settings (map centering and walk mode will be unavailable). Delete saved surveys in the App.

Updates will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
