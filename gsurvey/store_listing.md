## Full description (≤4000)
AcreTrack measures land quickly — built for farmers, plot buyers and surveyors.

MEASURE 4 WAYS
• Distance – two points, length in meters and feet
• Area + Perimeter – 3+ points; ideal for ploughing, seeding, lawns, fencing
• Circular Area – pick a center and radius point
• Walk & Add Points – walk the boundary; auto-adds a point every N feet, or tap to add each point manually

FEATURES
• Fullscreen Google Maps: Normal, Satellite, Terrain, Hybrid
• Area in m², acres, guntas, ares and sq ft
• Side lengths in meters and feet with directions (N, NE, E…)
• Lettered points A, B, C… with draggable markers
• Address of the plot via reverse geocoding
• Search address, coordinates or Google Maps links
• Open shared Maps links and AcreTrack plot links
• Share plot image or link (WhatsApp, etc.)
• Export PDF reports
• Save surveys offline on your device

NOTE: Measurements are estimates, not legal surveys. Verify with a licensed surveyor for legal use.
